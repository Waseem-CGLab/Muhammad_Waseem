## 🔬 About Me

I'm a PhD student at Soonchunhyang University Computer Graphic, advised by Professor Hong Min. My research sits at the intersection of **physically based simulation**, **GPU parallel computing**, and **real-time rendering**, **Phycsical AI**, **Image Processing**.

**Currently working on:**  adaptive-particle boiling / phase-change simulation for Meta Quest/ Physical AI
**Looking to collaborate on:** neural-accelerated solvers, haptics-coupled soft-tissue sim
**Ask me about:** SPH/PBF/XPBD, GPU atomics, spatial hashing, Unity compute shaders, AI.

## 🧪 Research Interests

| Area | Topics |
|------|--------|
| **Fluids** | SPH (WCSPH / DFSPH / IISPH), Position-Based Fluids, XPBD fluids, multiphase & free-surface flow |
| **Thermal / Phase Change** | Boiling, bubble nucleation & growth, evaporation, heat transfer between particles |
| **Deformables** | XPBD cloth, soft bodies, **surgical tissue** (cutting, suturing, cautery) |
| **Performance** | Adaptive particle sizing / splitting-merging, parallel constraint solvers, async compute, LOD |
| **VR Rendering** | Screen-space fluid rendering, foveated rendering, dynamic resolution, photoreal shading |
| **Validation** | Matching real-world physics (surface tension, viscosity, buoyancy, thermal conductivity) |
